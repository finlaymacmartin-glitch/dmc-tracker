# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person: the owner-operator of Delisle Mowing Company (trade-school grad, first business). He uses the app on his iPhone standing in driveways, often in bright sunlight, sometimes with work gloves. Sessions are 10-60 seconds: log a visit, check who owes, add an expense. His mentor (Finlay) uses exports on a PC for accounting counsel; the app itself is built for the operator.

## Product Purpose

Replace paper notes with a zero-cost, offline-first tracker for clients, contracts, AR, visits, quotes, expenses, mileage, and equipment. Success = every dollar owed is visible and chased, every expense is captured for tax time, and the winter snowplow line gets a real P&L. Data lives only on his device; a JSON export feeds an Excel workbook.

## Brand Personality

Dependable, plain-spoken, hardworking. It should feel like well-maintained equipment: sturdy, clean, nothing decorative that doesn't earn its keep. Confidence comes from clarity (big legible numbers, obvious next actions), not slickness.

## Anti-references

- Dark-mode SaaS dashboards (user explicitly rejected; unreadable in sunlight)
- Ultra-minimal grayscale apps (rejected; too quiet for outdoor glance-reads)
- Generic bootstrap/admin-template look; cream/beige "AI default" surfaces
- Anything requiring precision taps: targets must work with cold or gloved thumbs

## Design Principles

1. **Sunlight first.** High contrast, solid surfaces, no subtle gray-on-gray; if it can't be read on a phone at noon in a driveway, it fails.
2. **One thumb, ten seconds.** The daily actions (log visit, record payment, add expense) are one or two large taps from open.
3. **Numbers carry the hierarchy.** Money owed and money spent are the loudest things on any screen.
4. **Trust through steadiness.** Consistent components, no surprise layouts; the app should feel identical every day, like a tool that's always where he left it.
5. **Offline is the normal case.** Nothing may look broken without a connection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG mandate, but target AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body text) because the sunlight requirement demands it anyway. Touch targets ≥44px. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Single-language (English), CAD currency.
